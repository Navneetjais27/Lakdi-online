How to play
  1. Before each match, 4 cards are opened at a time until a Joker (Jack) appears. Its suit is trump for all 13 rounds.
  2. Everyone gets 13 cards. Then each player says their hand: how many of the 13 rounds they think they will win (1–13).
  3. In each round, follow the suit that was led. If you have a bigger card of that suit than the biggest one thrown so far, you must throw a bigger one. If you don't have that suit, you can cut with a trump.
  4. Highest trump wins the round; otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins. The winner leads next.
  5. Scoring: if you win at least what you said, you get 10 × what you said, plus 1 for each extra round (said 2, won 4 → 22). If you win fewer, you get minus 10 × what you said (said 2, won 1 → −20).
